DI-Showcase

Minimal working example of a test corpus for showcasing the potential of TEIKiss in the context of presenting TEI-annotated research data.

  • All files could be stored in a public github-repo and thereby made available for download.
  • The search implemented is linked to the dstar search engine of the Zentrum Sprache at Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities. As the working example is not indexed, the search here has functional means only.
  • Finally, direct access to the texts is made possible via an advanced XML2HTML-transformation.

Download

GitHub: Korpus DI-Showcase

Search

Corpus deployment allows complex search queries (dstar-interface) based on linguistic pre-analysis (lemmatizing, POS tagging, orthographical normalization), corpus analysis with Wortverlaufskurven as well as a diachronic collocation analysis via DiaCollo.

Access to the texts

Choose one of the files below by following the link. This will open an automatically generated text-view of the work based on the annotations within the XML-files. A view buttons on the foot of the right hand side allow for more insights:

  • toggle TOC will show a table of content (if available)
  • toggle dark mode will switch between background color from dark to light and vice versa
  • show header will open the TEIHeader and will show the bibliographical metadata of the text
  • show facimiles (if available) will load the facsimiles of the digitized book
